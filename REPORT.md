**Problem Statement**

	For this design project, our group defined the problem to be the process of planning and designing, then creating and thoroughly testing a Model View Controller website using proper industry standard testing practices covered in class. Constraints also apply, which we must consider and follow several of them; these include: Economic factors, Regulatory compliance, Reliability, Sustainability and Environmental Factors, Ethics, and Societal Impacts. Due to the nature of the project, the focus of the project is to gain practical experience using several different testing and validation techniques, including Boundary value testing, Equivalence class testing, Decision tables testing, State transition testing, and Use case testing. As well, two functions must be tested; using Path testing and Data flow. During this process, we will follow the structured engineering design process; such as considering requirements, research, and brainstorming. Including analyzing and iterating on potential functions and prototypes. One important idea we are considering is whether to have accounts, which has a large upside, and some downside to implementing, and our process will prototype and test it. Our approved topic/idea for this project is Group Leader, a scheduling app for groups. Each member adds their schedule and availability, then the program will find common times between them, allowing the group to all pick a preferred time. This has a lot of use cases, including just for friend groups to meet, or even in workplaces, when they would need to schedule a meeting. During the development cycle, we will document this process thoroughly, including test cases we use, commenting code, and having a public github. Delegating work is another area for this project, where we will track which group member contributes individually.
